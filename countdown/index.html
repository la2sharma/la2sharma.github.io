Why I am counting

Toronto is home now. It gave me a career, a routine, and winters that teach you patience. But some hungers do not translate. You cannot DoorDash the smell of a tandoor at 7 in the morning, or the sound of a steel plate landing on a wooden table with butter melting into every crack of a kulcha.

So I booked the flight. Toronto to Doha, Doha to Amritsar, wheels down in the dark at 2:30 in the morning. I will probably be the most awake person on that plane, because every hour closer is one hour closer to home.

This page is my countdown, and my promise to myself: the first morning back, no meetings, no plans, no sensible schedule. Just the city, the food, and the people I miss.
